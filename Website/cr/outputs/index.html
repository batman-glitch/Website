04 — A few details

Good to know.

A simple process keeps the focus where it belongs: on the record.

Can we work together remotely?

Yes. Share files through your preferred transfer service, and we can keep notes and revisions organized by email or a shared review link.

What should I send for a first conversation?

A rough bounce, a short description of the direction, your references, and a target release date are a great start. You do not need to have everything figured out.

How do revisions work?

We agree on the revision plan before the project begins. Feedback is gathered into clear notes so each pass moves the mix forward.

Can you work from a stereo bounce?

For mastering, yes. For a full mix, multitrack stems or a session export give us more room to shape the sound.
